# Guess the Movie

A movie quiz game in a Jupyter notebook. Players have 60 seconds to guess as many movies as they can from film stills and clues. 

Sound effects and the ending song are made with Google's Lyria 3 music model.

## How to play

1. Type your name and press **Start**.
2. Each question shows a movie screenshot or a clue, with 4 options. Click the right movie.
3. Screenshot question = 1 point. Clue question = 2 points. A wrong answer gives no points. 
4. When time runs out, the game shows your name and score, and plays an ending song:
   - 0 to 10 points: a mocking song
   - 11 to 20 points: a congratulations song
   - 21 points and up: a heavenly "legendary" song
